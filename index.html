Founder, AsymmetrIQ Labs

I run a quantitative research laboratory working at the intersection of machine learning and financial markets. The question underneath the work is a general one: can a system learn to act sensibly in an environment that is adversarial, non-stationary, and unforgiving of overconfidence?

I came to it from institutional analysis rather than from engineering, and that shows in what I find interesting about it. The part of an autonomous system I care most about is not what it can do. It is what it is not permitted to do, and whether that limit holds when the system is confident.
